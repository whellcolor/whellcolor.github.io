1. Texture Synthesis & Pixel Reconstruction Matrix

WCC CleanWM utilizes a fast **Image Inpainting Engine** executing directly on your localized client-side browser thread. When a user highlights text or a logo, the engine isolates the targeted structural mask coordinates. It analyzes surrounding texture profiles, geometric alignments, and ambient lighting channels.

By interpolating high-frequency pixel boundaries, the algorithm replaces the mask area with synthesized patterns. This guarantees a seamless integration that fills structural voids flawlessly, leaving no blurred borders or digital artifacts.
