1.1 Mathematical Reward Distribution Model

We can model the net profitability $\Pi$ of a high-APY liquidity pool relative to native token emissions and gas expenditure using the following equation:

$$\Pi = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \left( \frac{\text{Principal} \cdot \text{APY}_t}{365} \right) - \mathcal{L}_{\text{impermanent}} - \gamma_{\text{gas}}$$
