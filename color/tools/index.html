Step-by-Step Tutorial: Generating and Implementing Custom Palettes

  1. Analyze Project Requirements: Determine whether your application requires a Coolors Design Hub vibe or a darker tone typical of blockchain tools.
  2. Select Your Palette Generator: Use the interactive generator above or explore external libraries like the Color Hunt Community for pre-made combinations.
  3. Export Hex Codes: Copy the generated HEX, RGB, or HSL values directly into your CSS layout variables.
